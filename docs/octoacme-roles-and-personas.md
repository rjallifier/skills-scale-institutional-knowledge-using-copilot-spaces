# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery / Program Coordinators

### Role Summary
Delivery or Program Coordinators support Project Managers by tracking schedules, dependencies, decisions, and follow-up across teams. They help keep delivery work visible but do not replace the Project Manager's accountability for the project plan.

### Responsibilities
- Track milestones, cross-team dependencies, and action items
- Keep project boards, status notes, and decision records current
- Surface delays or unresolved dependencies to the Project Manager
- Coordinate logistics for planning sessions and delivery check-ins

### Goals
- Make handoffs and commitments visible across teams
- Help teams resolve blockers before they affect milestones

### Typical Communication
- Regular check-ins with the Project Manager and delivery teams
- Dependency and action-item updates in project boards
- Escalations to the Project Manager when owners or dates are at risk

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical decisions and ensure proposed solutions fit the system architecture and integration needs. They partner with Developers without replacing their ownership of implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Provide technical direction and review designs for feasibility
- Identify architecture, integration, scalability, and technical risks
- Help Developers break down work and estimate technical effort
- Advise the Product Manager and Project Manager on technical trade-offs and impacts
- Coordinate technical validation with QA/Testing

### Goals
- Deliver secure, maintainable solutions that meet product needs
- Reduce integration risk and avoid preventable rework

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical documentation with Developers
- Planning and risk discussions with the Project Manager and Product Manager
- Testability and integration planning with QA/Testing

---

## Operations / Release Managers

### Role Summary
Operations or Release Managers coordinate operational readiness and release activities, working with the delivery team to deploy and verify changes safely.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release plans, deployment windows, and readiness checks
- Confirm monitoring, rollback, and operational handoff plans are prepared
- Track deployment and post-release verification actions
- Coordinate incident response and notify the appropriate on-call or support teams
- Advise the Project Manager on release risks and readiness

### Goals
- Make releases predictable and reduce production disruption
- Ensure deployed changes can be monitored, supported, and rolled back

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews with the Project Manager, Technical Lead, Developers, and QA/Testing
- Deployment coordination with operations and on-call teams
- Release announcements and verification updates with Product Managers and Support

---

## Support / Customer Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Support or Customer Success Representatives bring customer needs and operational feedback into project planning and help prepare teams to support delivered changes.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer issues, feedback, and impact with the Product Manager
- Help assess customer impact and prioritize support-related risks
- Review release notes and prepare support guidance for upcoming changes
- Route post-release issues to Developers and the Project Manager with relevant details

### Goals
- Improve customer outcomes and continuity of support during releases
- Ensure customer feedback informs product decisions and follow-up work

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback and issue reviews with the Product Manager
- Release and support-readiness coordination with the Project Manager and Operations / Release Manager
- Defect triage with Developers and QA/Testing

---

## Security / Compliance Reviewers

### Role Summary
Security or Compliance Reviewers assess applicable security, privacy, and regulatory requirements and advise the team on risks and necessary controls. They provide review and recommendations; designated project and product owners remain accountable for delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and compliance requirements during planning
- Review designs, changes, and evidence for relevant risks or control gaps
- Recommend mitigations and track required follow-up with responsible owners
- Escalate significant risks through established security or compliance channels

### Goals
- Address security and compliance risks before release
- Make required controls and evidence clear to the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Requirement and risk discussions with the Product Manager and Project Manager
- Design and implementation reviews with Technical Leads and Developers
- Verification and evidence coordination with QA/Testing and Operations / Release Managers
